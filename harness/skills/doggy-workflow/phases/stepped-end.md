**Ending a session.** Ending before the last step is done, at a step boundary or mid-step, is a nonterminal stop: the spec stays `active` and Workflow item 9 applies. Before the closing report, append to `# Session Memory` what the next session would otherwise rediscover by repeating work or making a wrong move:

- *Failed Attempts & Reasons*: an approach that was implemented, run, or investigated and failed, and that a later session could plausibly try again, with its evidence and whether the cause is verified or a hypothesis. The execution ledger stays the verification loop's working log; a row here is the part a new session needs, with the evidence and re-check the ledger lacks.
- *Decisions & Reasons & Alternatives*: a choice main made during the work that is neither an engineer call nor another brief item, why, and the option not taken. An engineer call goes into `# Assumptions and Defaults` and `# Alternatives` by amendment instead.
- *Unresolved Questions & Uncertainties*: what is unknown or unconfirmed about the state of the work (an unverified cause, a behavior seen once, a check not yet run), what is known so far, and what would settle it. A question that affects a requirement, an engineer call, or a step goes into `# Open Questions` by amendment instead.

Every row is objective and reproducible: an observation, evidence a reader can re-run or open (a command and its output, a `path:line`, a commit), and how to re-check it. No recommendations, preferences, or instructions, and no row that only repeats what the code, git history, or another section of the spec shows. Append rows, never edit them; when a row no longer holds, add a new row saying so. Write `none` in a section with nothing to add. These sections serve this task only: they are archived with the spec, and what should outlive the task goes through the closing report into `facts.md` or try-n-fail.
