**Completion questions.** While drafting the spec, before the approval brief, write into `# Completion Questions` 4 to 5 questions about the result as it will exist when the spec is done, each with its expected answer and what it tests. They follow the rules above; as a set they cover the trade-off, at least one engineer call, and the final shape, with at most one question about a failure or boundary path. Do not show them in the approval brief. A draft revision after the approval quiz replaces the questions it invalidates and never edits an expected answer. After approval only an amendment changes them; an amendment that adds requirements (a held decision's step, for example) adds the questions they need.
