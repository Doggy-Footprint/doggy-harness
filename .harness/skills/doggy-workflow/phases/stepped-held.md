**Held decisions.** A *Your decision* that no earlier step depends on may be held for its step. Until that step starts, carry it in the spec as an open question naming the step, name it under the step's `held decisions`, and write none of the requirements that depend on it. When the step starts, ask it in a decision round, then add the dependent requirements, Cases, and obligations to that step by an amendment the user approves through a re-brief that includes the picks comparison.
